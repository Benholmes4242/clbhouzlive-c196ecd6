Do NOT damp `circle_round`, cap cards per course, or lower the club ring weight to
reduce "flooding" of one club on `/amateur`.

Measured Sep 2026: 11 of the 12 club-assigned members with rounds are at Sundridge
Park (Hanbury Manor 1, all others none). Control: David Lang (Hanbury Manor, 308
rounds, only member at his club) has ZERO `club` ring cards; his top 20 is records,
notable rounds and his own play — what the design intends.

**Why:** the ring model is correct. The flood is an artefact of an unrepresentative
member base and resolves itself as the base diversifies; any of those levers would
tune away the most relevant content on the page and become actively wrong later.
Same trap as the handicap threshold: a measurement taken on an unrepresentative base.
